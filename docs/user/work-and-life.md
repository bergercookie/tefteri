# Separate work and life

You can label your items as Work or Life. Then you can look at only one kind at a time.
This page tells you how to label items and how to use the switch.

## What you can label

You can label tasks, calendar entries and notes. You can also label a whole container.
A container is a Vikunja project, a Google calendar or a group of notes.

When you label a container, all items in it get that label. This includes new items that you sync later.

## Label a container

1. Open Settings.
2. Choose the connection.
3. Find the container in the list.
4. Choose Life or Work for the container.

Tefteri saves the label in tefteri. It does not change your app.

## Label one item

1. Choose the item.
2. Choose Label.
3. Choose Life or Work.

A label on one item is stronger than the label of its container. The item keeps its own label.
To go back to the label of the container, choose Label and then Use container label.

## Use the switch

The switch is at the top of every screen. It has three choices:

- All. You see all items.
- Life. You see only Life items.
- Work. You see only Work items.

1. Choose All, Life or Work at the top of the screen.
2. Look at the daily view, the calendar and the notes.

The choice applies to tasks, calendar entries and notes in all views. Tefteri keeps your choice when you change screens.

## The unclassified marker

An item with no label has the status unclassified. It shows under All. It has a marker that tells you it has no label.
It does not show under Life or Work.

To remove the marker, give the item a label. Or give a label to its container.
To find unclassified items, choose All and look for the marker.
