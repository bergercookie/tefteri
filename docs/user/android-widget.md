# Open a view from your Android home screen

The tefteri widget puts four buttons on your home screen. Each button opens a view of tefteri at
once. This page tells you how to add the widget and what the buttons do.

## What the buttons do

The widget is one row with four buttons:

- Today opens the plan of the day.
- Week opens the plan of the week.
- Month opens the monthly theme plan. Read [Plan your month](monthly-planning.md).
- Planner opens the planner. It shows your tasks and your calendar side by side. Use it to drag
  tasks onto the calendar.

The widget uses the colors of the app. It follows the light theme or the dark theme of your phone.

## Add the widget to your home screen

1. Press and hold an empty place on your home screen.
2. Choose Widgets.
3. Find tefteri in the list and choose Tefteri shortcuts.
4. Drag the widget to a place on your home screen. The widget is four cells wide and one cell
   high.

On some phones, you press and hold the tefteri icon and choose Widgets to find it faster.

You can make the widget wider. Press and hold the widget, and drag its edge.

## Use the widget

Choose a button. Tefteri opens the view of the button. If tefteri is already open, it moves to
that view. If you closed tefteri, it starts in that view.

You stay signed in when you come back to tefteri. If your session ended, tefteri asks you to sign
in first. After you sign in, choose the button again.

## If the widget does not show

- Check that you installed the newest version of tefteri.
- Restart your phone, and look for tefteri in the list of widgets again.

## The agenda widget

The agenda widget shows the events and the tasks of one day on your home screen. Use it to see
your day without you opening tefteri.

### What the agenda widget shows

- The events of the day come first, in time order. Each event shows its time and its place. The
  color bar at the left is the color of the calendar.
- The tasks that are due on the day come next. Open tasks are before tasks that you did. An open
  task has an empty box, and a done task has a check mark and a lighter color. A task shows its
  due time, or "Task" when it has a day but no time. The color bar is the color of the project.
- The arrow at the left shows the day before. The arrow at the right shows the day after. You can
  go from one day to the next as many times as you want.
- The name between the arrows shows the day that you look at, for example "Today" and "22 Nov".
  For the day before and the day after, it shows "Yesterday" and "Tomorrow". For other days, it
  shows the name of the weekday. Choose the name to go back to today.
- The refresh button loads the day again.

On today, the list starts at the first event that is not over. The events that are over are above
it, in a lighter color, and you can scroll up to see them. An event that is on now is not over. If
every event is over, the list starts at the tasks. If no event is over yet, the list starts at the
top. The list scrolls one time each time that you come to today, for example when you choose the
date to go back to today. It does not scroll again when the widget loads the day again. Other days
show from the top, and a day that has passed shows all its events in the lighter color. An all-day
event of today is not over, and the events that are over are always before it, so that they are
out of view.

The widget shows the current day when you add it. It also goes back to the current day when a new
day starts. It does not stay on a day that you chose earlier.

Choose an event or a task to open the planner in tefteri. The planner shows the day that the
widget shows.

### Add the agenda widget

The agenda widget is a second widget. It does not replace the shortcuts widget, and a widget that
is already on your home screen does not change. You must add the agenda widget from the list of
widgets.

1. Press and hold an empty place on your home screen.
2. Choose Widgets.
3. Find tefteri in the list and choose Tefteri agenda.
4. Drag the widget to a place on your home screen. It is four cells wide and three cells high.
   You can make it larger.

If you do not find Tefteri agenda in the list, search the list for "agenda". If it is not there,
restart your phone. Some launchers keep an old list of widgets until they restart.

### Sign in first

The widget uses the same server and the same login as the app. Sign in to tefteri one time. Then
the widget shows your day. If the widget shows "Sign in to Tefteri", open the app and sign in.
If the widget shows that it could not load the agenda, check your network and choose the refresh
button.
