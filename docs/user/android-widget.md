# Open a view from your Android home screen

The tefteri widget puts four buttons on your home screen. Each button opens a view of tefteri at
once. This page tells you how to add the widget and what the buttons do.

## What the buttons do

The widget is one row with four buttons:

- Today opens the plan of the day.
- Week opens the plan of the week.
- Month opens the monthly theme plan. Read [Plan your month](monthly-planning.md).
- Planner opens the planner. It shows your tasks and your calendar side by side. Use it to drag
  tasks onto the calendar.

The widget uses the colors of the app. It follows the light theme or the dark theme of your phone.

## Add the widget to your home screen

1. Press and hold an empty place on your home screen.
2. Choose Widgets.
3. Find tefteri in the list and choose Tefteri shortcuts.
4. Drag the widget to a place on your home screen. The widget is four cells wide and one cell
   high.

On some phones, you press and hold the tefteri icon and choose Widgets to find it faster.

You can make the widget wider. Press and hold the widget, and drag its edge.

## Use the widget

Choose a button. Tefteri opens the view of the button. If tefteri is already open, it moves to
that view. If you closed tefteri, it starts in that view.

You stay signed in when you come back to tefteri. If your session ended, tefteri asks you to sign
in first. After you sign in, choose the button again.

## If the widget does not show

- Check that you installed the newest version of tefteri.
- Restart your phone, and look for tefteri in the list of widgets again.
