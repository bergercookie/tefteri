# Solve conflicts

Sometimes you and another app change the same item. Tefteri finds this and asks you what to do.
Tefteri never replaces an item unless you choose it.

## What a conflict is

A conflict happens when two changes to the same item do not agree. For example, you change the title of a task in tefteri.
Then, before you sync, you change the title of the same task in Vikunja.

The app has one version of the item. Tefteri has a different version. Tefteri cannot know which version you want.

## How you find a conflict

After a sync, tefteri shows a conflict mark next to each connection with a conflict.
The Conflicts screen lists all items with a conflict.

1. Press Sync.
2. Read the result for each connection.
3. If a connection shows a conflict, choose Conflicts.

Other items sync as usual. A conflict in one item does not stop the other items.

## Compare the two versions

1. On the Conflicts screen, choose an item.
2. Read the Local version. This is the version in tefteri.
3. Read the Remote version. This is the version in your app.
4. Look at the fields that are different. Tefteri marks them.

## Choose which version to keep

You have two choices:

- Keep local. Tefteri sends its version to your app. The version in your app changes.
- Keep remote. Tefteri takes the version from your app. The version in tefteri changes.

To choose:

1. Choose the item on the Conflicts screen.
2. Choose Keep local or Keep remote.
3. Choose Confirm.

The item has one version now. The conflict is gone from the list.

## Rules that tefteri follows

- Tefteri does not solve a conflict for you.
- Tefteri does not replace a version until you choose.
- If you do not choose, both versions stay as they are.
- A failed sync does not remove your changes.

You can leave a conflict and come back later. The item stays in the list.
If the app does not answer when you choose, tefteri tells you. Read [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
